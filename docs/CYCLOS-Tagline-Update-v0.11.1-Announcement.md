# CYCLOS Website Update v0.11.1 — Tagline Change & Translation Review Request

**Prepared by:** Computer Solutions SA (Greece)
**Date:** 26 May 2026
**Version:** 0.11.1
**Classification:** Project Documentation (WP5 — Dissemination)

---

## Summary

Following Sophie's note on the partner thread (20 May 2026), the project tagline
has been changed from **"Youth Cycling for Inclusion"** to **"Cycling for
Inclusion"** across the website and project materials. The shorter form is more
inclusive and avoids implying the initiative is limited to young people only.

The change is **live** at https://cyclos-project.eu and released as version
**0.11.1**.

> Note: This affects the **tagline/branding** only. The project acronym CYCLOS,
> its mission (which remains focused on disadvantaged youth), and the registered
> ERASMUS+ project are unchanged. Descriptive sentences that mention "inclusion
> of young people" in body text were intentionally left as they are.
