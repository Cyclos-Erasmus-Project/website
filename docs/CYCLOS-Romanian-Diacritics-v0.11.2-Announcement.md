# CYCLOS Website Update v0.11.2 — Romanian Diacritics Correction (Partners Section)

**Prepared by:** Computer Solutions SA (Greece)
**Date:** 28 May 2026
**Version:** 0.11.2
**Classification:** Project Documentation (WP5 — Dissemination)

---

## Summary

Following Andra Barboloviciu's review of the Romanian site (ASSOC, email of
27 May 2026), the **Partners section** text has been corrected to use proper
Romanian diacritics. The previous version used plain-ASCII characters
(e.g. "Lucram impreuna…"), which is grammatically incorrect Romanian.

The corrected text supplied by ASSOC is now **live** at https://cyclos-project.eu
and released as version **0.11.2**.

> Note: The official ASSOC legal name ("Asociatia Profesionala Neguvernamentala
> de Asistenta Sociala") in the partner profile was intentionally left without
> diacritics, matching the organization's own registered form. Please let us know
> if you would prefer the diacritics version there as well.

---

## What changed

The Partners-section strings in the Romanian translation now read:

| Key | Before | After |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Partnership CTA heading | Lucram impreuna in toata Europa | **Lucrăm împreună în toată Europa** |
| Partnership CTA description | Parteneriatul nostru divers reuneste expertiza in promovarea sanatatii, servicii sociale, educatie si tehnologie pentru a crea un impact semnificativ pentru tinerii defavorizati. | **Parteneriatul nostru divers reunește expertiză în promovarea sănătății, servicii sociale, educație și tehnologie pentru a crea un impact semnificativ pentru tinerii defavorizați.** |
| "Areas of expertise" label | Domenii de expertiza | **Domenii de expertiză** |

The third item was found during a full re-scan of the Romanian translation block
for the same class of issue and corrected alongside the two ASSOC flagged.

Quick link to verify on the live site:
- RO Partners page: https://cyclos-project.eu/ro/partners/
